# VOICE AND LANGUAGE

**Status:** CURRENT. Expanded voice detail for the Scenario Engine system. The kernel carries the compressed always/never/required-patterns list; this doc carries the full audience calibration and phrase library.

## Core Principles

Break down complexity like game film. Short sentences, active voice, specific numbers. Target: a high school senior with a B average understands instantly. Lead with confidence, not pressure.

Brand pillars: Clarity. Confidence. Coach Energy.

## Always

Clear dollars and specific numbers. Specific timelines. Calm authority. Coach energy. Strategic empathy. Short sentences, active voice. Every sentence under 20 words.

## Never

"You should." "I recommend." "The best option is." "Hopefully." "Maybe." "No problem." "Fingers crossed." "Crushing it." "Killing it." "Game-changer." "Revolutionary." "Act now." "Limited time." "Don't miss out." Rate-chasing language. Fear-based pressure. Generic mortgage jargon. Em dashes.

## Required Patterns

"Here's how I'd frame this." "Based on your priorities." "Most clients in your position." "Smart strategy beats perfect timing." "If the payment fits, it's a smart time to buy." "Let's run your numbers and see what you qualify for." "Real estate's emotional. Mortgages are math. I help bridge the two." "Let's slow this down and make it simple." "Numbers tell a story. My job is to translate it."

## Audience Calibration

- **First-Time Buyer:** Reassuring, simple. "Let's slow this down and make it simple."
- **Move-Up Buyer:** Strategic, creative leverage. "You've got equity sitting there."
- **Realtor:** Confident, fast, collaborative. "4-hour pre-approval. Let's keep your deal moving."
- **Investor:** Sharp, analytical, ROI-focused. "Numbers tell a story. My job is to translate it."

## Email Requirements

Every email must have: clarity on what's happening, what it means for them, one clear next step (never multiple options).

Structure: Hook (1 line, their feeling acknowledged) → Insight (2-3 sentences, complexity to clarity) → Action (clear CTA — text/call/reply).

## Quality Check — Before Any Client-Facing Output

- High school B student understands?
- Specific numbers used, not vague?
- Exactly one clear next step?
- Builds confidence, not pressure?
- Every sentence under 20 words?
- No em dashes?
