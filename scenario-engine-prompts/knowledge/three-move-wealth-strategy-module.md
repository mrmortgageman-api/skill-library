# THREE-MOVE WEALTH STRATEGY FRAME

**Status:** CURRENT. Ported unchanged from the 2026-06-03 module set, restored into the 2026-08-20 kernel rebuild.

**Trigger:** Borrower articulates a multi-transaction real estate plan, mentions a future purchase after this one, plans to convert primary to rental, or asks "does this deal max us out." Activates during the Strategy Interpretation layer of the B2C Notion Handoff Block. Informs Advisory Notes and Decision Points sections.

When a borrower's plan extends beyond the current transaction, map each move explicitly before modeling Move 1.

## Three-Move Framework

- **Move 1 — Current Transaction:** What they are buying now. Full scenario modeling applies. All Enforcement Layer rules apply.
- **Move 2 — Mid-Term Move:** Next property acquisition or major financial repositioning. Model the DTI impact of Move 1 on Move 2 capacity. Flag borrowing constraints created by this transaction.
- **Move 3 — Long-Term Position:** Ultimate portfolio or wealth destination. Show how Move 1 and Move 2 build toward it. Identify what must go right for Move 3 to work.

## Required Outputs When This Frame Activates

- DTI before Move 1
- DTI after Move 1 closes
- Estimated DTI capacity remaining for Move 2
- Income events required to unlock Move 2 (rate drops, rental income growth, pay increase)
- Timeline estimate for Move 2 readiness based on current trajectory
- Strategic risks that could block Move 3

**Calculator authority:** the three DTI figures above are the same figures `dti-stress-test-module.md` produces — this module frames them, it doesn't calculate them independently. Run the DTI Stress Test Module's Snapshot 1/2/3 math (which is itself subordinated to the Calculator Router) and carry those results in here rather than deriving a second, parallel DTI figure. See the kernel's Module Math Subordination Rule.

## Advisory Framing

"This deal is Move 1 of a three-part strategy. Here is what it sets up — and what it constrains."

Do not let the current transaction analysis crowd out the forward picture. The borrower needs both. Map the full game, not just the next play.
